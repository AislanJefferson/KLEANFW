# KLEANFW 0.1.4

Audit release of KLEANFW, a custom firmware for the **Quansheng UV-K1 /
UV-K5 V3 (PY32F071 + BK4829 only)**. It is a modified version of
[GOGUFW 3.6.2](https://github.com/Gogu-Qs/GOGUFW-UV-K1-Messenger) by Gogu-Qs
(F4HWN 6.3.0 base) and an independent project, not affiliated with or
endorsed by Gogu-Qs, F4HWN or Quansheng.

0.1.4 comes from an audit of every menu entry and of every field of the main
screen, plus three behavior changes: F+3 carries the frequency between MR
and VFO, the power key no longer cuts the received audio, and Spectrum PTT×2
leaves in the mode it was opened in. Everything in it was tested on a radio.

## Highlights

- **F+3 carries the frequency.** MR→VFO puts the active channel's exact
  frequency in that band's VFO. The channel's tones, offset, Reverse and
  scrambler are not copied; modulation, bandwidth, power and the other VFO
  settings stay those of the band's saved VFO. When the frequency is off the
  VFO's step grid, the VFO takes the channel's step (or 0.01 kHz). VFO→MR
  goes to the stored channel with the VFO frequency, else to the last MR
  channel as before.
- **Power key without audio cuts.** F+6 (or a side key set to POWER) briefly
  cut the received audio on every press, because it retuned the whole
  receiver. The new level now goes to TX only.
- **Spectrum PTT×2 keeps the mode.** Opened from the VFO, the peak always
  lands in the VFO like a typed frequency, even when an MR channel stores it;
  opened from MR, it still opens the peak channel.
- **Menu fixes.**
  - SetCtr/SetInv: leaving the menu with the submenu open (timeout, PTT, a
    popup) no longer keeps the previewed contrast or inversion on the LCD.
  - D Prel starts at 200 ms, the real minimum (30–190 ms all acted as
    200 ms), in the menu, the manual and the CHIRP module.
- **Main screen fixes** (all inherited from upstream):
  - CROSS BAND: while transmitting, the power bars show the level of the
    transmitting VFO, not the receiving one.
  - FULL RX RESPOND / MAIN TX FULL RX: `UNLOCK KEYBOARD` shows again when a
    locked key is pressed during a reception.
  - AM/USB: the step is shown in place of a CTCSS/DCS code, since tones only
    work in FM.
  - SetGUI TINY: the `USER` arrow no longer covers the 7th TX power bar of
    the transmitting VFO.
- **Manual:** the status bar table and the `SetMet`, `SetGUI`, `Mode` and
  `Compnd` entries were completed or corrected.

## Before you flash

- Back up the radio and its calibration with CHIRP and keep a DFU recovery
  image.
- First boot after updating: the version reset clears KEY_LOCK, MENU_LOCK,
  SET_KEY and SET_INV and turns `SetRxG` back ON.
- Use only the KLEANFW 0.1.4 CHIRP module (model **UV-K1 / UV-K5 V3 KLEANFW**).
  The EEPROM layout is the same as 0.1.3.
- Installed apps keep working (same app API). To install them, unzip
  `FM-Search-Apps.zip` and install both FM apps and Search.app with UV Studio
  (Apps page). GitHub does not accept `.app` attachments, so the apps come
  zipped.
