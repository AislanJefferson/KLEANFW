# KLEANFW 0.2.1

HOP follow-up release of KLEANFW, a custom firmware for the **Quansheng
UV-K1 / UV-K5 V3 (PY32F071 + BK4829 only)**. It is a modified version of
[GOGUFW 3.6.2](https://github.com/Gogu-Qs/GOGUFW-UV-K1-Messenger) by Gogu-Qs
(F4HWN 6.3.0 base) and an independent project, not affiliated with or
endorsed by Gogu-Qs, F4HWN or Quansheng.

A HOP receiver needs `MsgRx` ON and `VOX` OFF. 0.2.1 sets both by itself
while HOP is active. Everything else is the same as 0.2.0.

> **HOP works between 0.2.0 and 0.2.1 radios.** A 0.2.0 receiver still
> needs `MsgRx` ON and `VOX` OFF set by hand. HOP is not compatible with
> 0.1.4 and earlier.

## Highlights

- **HOP turns MsgRx ON and VOX OFF.** The receiver reads the HOP seed and
  resync bursts through the Messenger FSK receiver, which `MsgRx` OFF or
  `VOX` ON keep off. While HOP is active, the radio sets `MsgRx` = ON and
  `VOX` = OFF and saves them as soon as it is idle (no RX or TX):
  - HOP is active when the channel being received or transmitted on is HOP,
    or VFO A or B with Dual/Full Watch, outside a scan;
  - meanwhile the `MsgRx` and `VOX` menus and the VOX action (side key,
    hold 7) refuse with the double beep (silent with `Beep` OFF);
  - both stay so after HOP is off: change them again if you want;
  - with `MsgRx` ON, the radio receives messages again and, with `MsgAck`
    or `RngRsp` ON, answers ACK and PONG on any channel.
- **Battery save** now also stays awake in Cross Band when only the TX
  channel is HOP.

## Before you flash

- Back up the radio and its calibration with CHIRP and keep a DFU recovery
  image.
- First boot after updating: the version reset clears KEY_LOCK, MENU_LOCK,
  SET_KEY and SET_INV and turns `SetRxG` back ON.
- Use only the KLEANFW 0.2.1 CHIRP module (model **UV-K1 / UV-K5 V3 KLEANFW**).
  The EEPROM layout is the same as 0.2.0.
- Installed apps keep working (same app API); the apps are byte-identical to
  those of 0.2.0. To install them, unzip `FM-Search-Apps.zip` and install
  both FM apps and Search.app with UV Studio (Apps page). GitHub does not
  accept `.app` attachments, so the apps come zipped.

## Checks on a radio

The change was checked by code review and builds only; it is not
radio-tested yet. Useful checks, with reports in
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/AislanJefferson/KLEANFW/issues):

1. HOP channel with `VOX` ON and `MsgRx` OFF saved, radio idle: the VOX icon
   disappears, the menu shows `MsgRx` ON and `VOX` OFF, the menu and the VOX
   action give the double beep, and HOP syncs. A restart keeps the values.
2. Power on directly on a HOP channel with `VOX` ON and `Beep` OFF, without
   pressing a key: the VOX icon disappears and the receiver still opens on a
   signal.
3. Leave HOP: `MsgRx` and `VOX` stay ON and OFF and can be changed again.
4. VOX on a normal channel, from the menu and by holding 7: VOX keys the TX,
   the Messenger stops receiving, and messages arrive again after VOX is
   turned off.
5. Dual Watch with only B on HOP, Cross Band with only the TX on HOP, and
   Full Watch with HOP on a priority channel.
6. A scan over a HOP channel changes nothing until it stops. Holding UP/DOWN
   through an MR list onto a HOP channel saves once and keeps scrolling.
