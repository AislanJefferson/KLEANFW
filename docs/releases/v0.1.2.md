# KLEANFW 0.1.2

Maintenance release of KLEANFW, a custom firmware for the **Quansheng UV-K1 /
UV-K5 V3 (PY32F071 + BK4829 only)**. It is a modified version of
[GOGUFW 3.6.2](https://github.com/Gogu-Qs/GOGUFW-UV-K1-Messenger) by Gogu-Qs
(F4HWN 6.3.0 base) and an independent project, not affiliated with or
endorsed by Gogu-Qs, F4HWN or Quansheng.

0.1.2 brings back the HOP, Messenger, USB/serial, menu and radio fixes of the
withdrawn 0.2.0, without its Spectrum changes. On top of them come a BusyCL
fix, Spectrum fixes and a faster Spectrum handover, plus tooling that puts the
Quansheng stock firmware into a multiboot slot.

## Highlights

- **BusyCL blocks the PTT again.** On a busy channel the PTT shows BUSY and
  does not transmit, on simplex and repeater channels.
- **HOP scrambler**:
  - the RX follows the transmitter's clock rate between resyncs, so a clock
    difference between radios no longer drifts past the guard;
  - battery save does not sleep while a HOP channel waits for the seed
    burst;
  - a TX resync after a late tick uses the current slot.
- **Messenger**: ACK/PONG are only taken from the radio they were addressed
  to; RESEND of a failed message works; the HOP seed burst no longer leaves a
  stale capture timer; the channel lock no longer triggers a spurious
  dual-watch swap.
- **USB/serial**: bounded EEPROM reads, packets that end exactly at the end of
  the receive buffer, odd-size `0x051B` replies, and checked multiboot/app
  slot writes.
- **Menu and radio**:
  - keypad entry respects each menu's minimum;
  - `SetCtr` EXIT really cancels;
  - PriCh1/PriCh2 browsing works;
  - DCS badge and tone display are right;
  - the TX compander follows the TX VFO;
  - airband channels get no TX offset.
- **Spectrum (F+5)**:
  - sweep, keypad, RF and flash-wear fixes, such as no dbMax/trigger rewrite
    on every EXIT, no 0 Hz listen, the band edges and step changes, and the
    graph's last column and trigger line;
  - MR: key 4 (next list) recalibrates the scale and trigger level for the
    new list, as on entry;
  - experimental: after a transmission ends, the next one is found within
    ~45 ms instead of up to ~0.5 s (listening only; STILL unchanged).
- **Stock firmware in a multiboot slot** (tooling, not in the image):
  `tools/stockpatch/make_multiboot.sh <stock.bin> -m k1|k5v3` turns the
  Quansheng stock firmware (UV-K1 7.03.05 or UV-K5 V3 7.00.15) into a slot
  image:
  - it has the multiboot selector (MENU at power-on) and its own config bank,
    prepared once from the Main config with every stock menu field at its
    default;
  - the stock binary is not distributed: bring your own file;
  - voice prompts are not available in the slot: the multiboot uses the flash
    area of the factory voice pack.

## Before you flash

- Back up the radio and its calibration with CHIRP and keep a DFU recovery
  image.
- First boot after updating: the version reset clears KEY_LOCK, MENU_LOCK,
  SET_KEY and SET_INV and turns `SetRxG` back ON.
- Use only the KLEANFW 0.1.2 CHIRP module (model **UV-K1 / UV-K5 V3 KLEANFW**).
  The EEPROM layout is the same as 0.1.1.
- Installed apps keep working (same app API). To install them, unzip
  `FM-Search-Apps.zip` and install both FM apps and Search.app with UV Studio
  (Apps page). GitHub does not accept `.app` attachments, so the apps come
  zipped.

## Checks on a radio

The stock-firmware slot was tested on a UV-K1: it boots, the backlight and
keys work, and it keeps its own config bank. The firmware changes were checked
by code review and builds. Useful checks, with reports in
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/AislanJefferson/KLEANFW/issues):

1. With BusyCL ON, the PTT on a busy channel shows BUSY and does not transmit.
2. HOP between two radios over a long over stays clear after the resyncs.
3. Spectrum F+5: a new transmission after the previous one ends is heard
   quickly; EXIT does not rewrite the scale settings.
4. Messenger: messages, ACKs and RESEND between two radios.
